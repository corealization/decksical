# Use maths

For a drop from rest, ignoring air resistance, distance \(h\) after time \(t\) is:

\[
h = \frac{1}{2}gt^2
\]

On Earth, \(g \approx 9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\). After \(2\,\mathrm{s}\),
\(h \approx 19.6\,\mathrm{m}\).

!!! note "Why doesn't mass appear?"

    Gravity and inertia increase together with mass, so mass cancels out.
    Without air resistance, all objects fall at the same rate.
